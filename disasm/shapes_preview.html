Tailgunner: every picture in the ROM

Decoded from the program ROM by disasm/emit_shapes.py; the names are the labels of tailgunner_program_rom.asm. The models turn here for viewing only; in the game the fighters bank by their velocity.

Fighter models (MODELS)

Vertex lists of x, y, z bytes ($80 = 0, $FF ends) and edge lists of two vertex numbers per byte. NEWWAVE puts one of the four in each fighter's FTRn_MODEL.

Title letters (LETTERS)

The 3D models TITLE3D flies in during attract mode, same format. The four are drawn together from one origin, and the strokes are not split by letter: the R of GUNNER takes its upright from Letters 2 (vertices 14-15, $1A6A-$1A6D) and its bowl and leg from Letters 3. The single models below keep their place in that shared frame.

Test pattern (TESTFIG)

Drawn by TESTMODE when the game is powered on with START held.

Crosshair (XHAIRSHP) and shield (SHIELDPTS)

The shield is one path from the screen centre through the 12 points (screen positions, byte x 4): a lattice of diamonds across the whole screen. In the game each segment's brightness changes from frame to frame.

Vector font (CHRGRID / CHRSTROKES / CHRINDEX)

Each stroke joins two of 15 grid points (5 across, 3 down). PRINT maps '0'-'9', 'A'-'Z' and $5B (space).
